Contact

Questions, bugs and ideas

WatchBridge is made by one person, and every message is read. Ask a question, report something that doesn't work, or tell us what you would like it to do.

Prefer email? contacto@watchbridge.app

Before you write

  • Something doesn't work? The troubleshooting guide solves most cases (Accessibility switching itself off, Fire TV mode, reinstalling).
  • Reporting a bug? It helps to say which device you use (Google TV model or Fire TV), the app version (Settings) and what you pressed.
  • Ideas are welcome: say what you would do with it, not only the feature. Not everything fits, but every idea is read.

Your message (and your email, if you add one) is kept only to read and answer it. See the privacy policy.
