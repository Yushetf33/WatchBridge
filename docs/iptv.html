01Two more you'll use every day

Built for the remote, not for a mouse

Favorites

Your channels, first

Hold OK on any programme in a channel's row, choose “Add channel to favorites”, and a star marks it. One tap on the Group button shows only your favorites — out of hundreds of channels.

Multiview

Two channels at once

Hold OK on a channel while another one is playing and choose Multiview. Left and Right pick which one plays sound; OK takes one full screen. The live picture is blurred in this demo.

It needs a source that allows two streams at once. WatchBridge includes its own software decoder for MPEG-2 channels, so devices without one can show them too.

Search

Type it or say it, it tunes it

Press the magnifier and type a channel or what you want to watch — or press the microphone and say it (“put on BBC News”, “put on Men in Black”). WatchBridge looks it up in the TV guide, or lets you pick between the channels showing it. The live picture is blurred in this demo.

02And a lot more

Everything the IPTV guide does

Full EPG grid
Channels on the left, programmes on a timeline, now and next on every row and whatever is airing highlighted — it keeps up as the clock moves.
Catch-up and a longer guide
If your provider keeps an archive (M3U catch-up tags or Xtream Codes), press a past programme to watch it from the start. The guide reaches back up to 48 hours.
Favorites
A star marks them and one tap on Group shows only your favorites. Each list keeps its own.
Groups and categories
Filter the guide by the groups your list already has — Sports, Movies, News… — without leaving the grid.
Several lists
Keep as many M3U, Xtream Codes or file lists as you want and switch between them. Each list keeps its own favorites and locks.
Back to the last channel
In full screen, press OK twice (or your remote's last-channel key) to jump to the channel you were watching before.
Parental control with a PIN
Lock single channels or whole groups with a 4–8 digit PIN. Locked channels disappear from the guide, zapping and voice search until you enter it.
Programme alerts
Get a notification when a programme you picked is about to start.
Live football
Scores and match events right in the panel, and voice search that finds “the Madrid match”.
Open movies in your player
Hold OK on a movie or series in the guide to open it in Nuvio, Stremio, Plex or Jellyfin.
Channel actions on any programme
Hold OK on any block of a channel's row — not only its name — to add it to favorites, start Multiview or lock it.
Record programs
Hold OK on a programme in the guide and choose Record, or press the red button to record the channel you are watching. It saves to a USB drive or the TV's storage, and you watch it later from the Recordings screen. Scheduled recordings need the TV to be on.
Send a channel from your phone
Hold a channel in the WatchBridge Mobile app and send it to the TV: it opens on that channel.
Google TV and Fire TV
Works on Google TV and Android TV, and on Fire TV including older sticks.

05FAQ

IPTV questions

Is WatchBridge an alternative to TiviMate?

If you want a TV-style guide for your own M3U or Xtream Codes list on Google TV or Fire TV — an EPG grid, favorites, groups, several lists, catch-up, a parental PIN and Multiview — that is what the IPTV part of WatchBridge does. It is also an app that opens Google TV recommendations in Nuvio, Stremio, Plex or Jellyfin. WatchBridge is not affiliated with TiviMate. See the IPTV players compared side by side.

Does WatchBridge include TV channels or IPTV lists?

No. WatchBridge doesn't provide, host or sell any channels or lists — you bring your own, the same way you would in TiviMate or similar apps.

Which IPTV lists and guides does it accept?

An M3U/M3U8 link (also .m3u.gz and PLS), an Xtream Codes login (server, username and password) or an .m3u file sent from your phone or PC over Wi-Fi. The TV guide can be an XMLTV .xml or .xml.gz link, or the one your list already declares.

Does Multiview work on my TV?

Multiview shows two channels side by side. It needs a source that allows two streams at once and a device that can decode both; WatchBridge includes its own software decoder for MPEG-2 channels so it also works on devices without one. If your provider only allows one connection, the second channel won't open.

How does catch-up work?

If your provider keeps an archive (M3U lists with catch-up tags, or Xtream Codes panels), you press a programme that already aired and it plays from the start. The guide goes back up to 48 hours. It depends on your provider keeping that archive.

Which devices does the IPTV guide run on?

Google TV and Android TV devices, and Fire TV. It is included in WatchBridge TV on Google Play and in the APK.
