Everything the IPTV guide does

Full EPG grid

Channels on the left, programmes on a timeline, now and next on every row and whatever is airing highlighted — it keeps up as the clock moves.

Catch-up and a longer guide

If your provider keeps an archive (M3U catch-up tags or Xtream Codes), press a past programme to watch it from the start. The guide reaches back up to 48 hours.

Favorites

A star marks them and one tap on Group shows only your favorites. Each list keeps its own.

Groups and categories

Filter the guide by the groups your list already has — Sports, Movies, News… — without leaving the grid.

Several lists

Keep as many M3U, Xtream Codes or file lists as you want and switch between them. Each list keeps its own favorites and locks.

In full screen, press OK twice (or your remote's last-channel key) to jump to the channel you were watching before.

Parental control with a PIN

Lock single channels or whole groups with a 4–8 digit PIN. Locked channels disappear from the guide, zapping and voice search until you enter it.

FAQ

IPTV questions

Is WatchBridge an alternative to TiviMate?

If you want a TV-style guide for your own M3U or Xtream Codes list on Google TV or Fire TV — an EPG grid, favorites, groups, several lists, catch-up, a parental PIN and Multiview — that is what the IPTV part of WatchBridge does. It is also an app that opens Google TV recommendations in Nuvio, Stremio, Plex or Jellyfin. WatchBridge is not affiliated with TiviMate.

Does WatchBridge include TV channels or IPTV lists?

No. WatchBridge doesn't provide, host or sell any channels or lists — you bring your own, the same way you would in TiviMate or similar apps.

Which IPTV lists and guides does it accept?

An M3U/M3U8 link (also .m3u.gz and PLS), an Xtream Codes login (server, username and password) or an .m3u file sent from your phone or PC over Wi-Fi. The TV guide can be an XMLTV .xml or .xml.gz link, or the one your list already declares.

Does Multiview work on my TV?

Multiview shows two channels side by side. It needs a source that allows two streams at once and a device that can decode both; WatchBridge includes its own software decoder for MPEG-2 channels so it also works on devices without one. If your provider only allows one connection, the second channel won't open.

How does catch-up work?

If your provider keeps an archive (M3U lists with catch-up tags, or Xtream Codes panels), you press a programme that already aired and it plays from the start. The guide goes back up to 48 hours. It depends on your provider keeping that archive.

Which devices does the IPTV guide run on?

Google TV and Android TV devices, and Fire TV. It is part of the APK version of WatchBridge TV for now.
