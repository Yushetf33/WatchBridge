02What it does

Everything you use on the TV, one tap away

TV guide you can scroll
Channels on the left and programmes on a timeline, with what is on now and next. Search, groups, favorites and a "Continue watching" row with your last channels.
Live sports scores
Matches in the sports list show the score and the minute when they are covered, and while you watch football the player shows goals and yellow and red cards when you tap the screen.
Multiview
Watch up to 3 channels at once: one big and two small. Sound follows the screen you tap, and a double tap opens it full screen.
Send to your TV
Send a movie, a series or a live channel to your TV with one tap. It opens there on its own if WatchBridge TV is running.
Record on your TV
From the phone, ask your TV to record a channel for 30 minutes up to 3 hours. It saves on the TV and you watch it later.
Your library and recommendations
Movies, series, My list and history in sync with your TVs, and a detail screen for every title. Choose which app opens it: Nuvio, Stremio, Plex, Jellyfin and more.

05FAQ

Questions

Is WatchBridge Mobile free?

It has a free 2-day trial with no card, then a single payment of €4.99 — the same account and the same payment as WatchBridge TV, so you do not pay twice.

Does WatchBridge Mobile include TV channels?

No. WatchBridge does not host or provide any channels or content: you add your own M3U link or Xtream Codes login from your provider, and the app shows its channels with a TV guide.

Does it work on tablets?

Yes. It is the same app for Android phones and tablets (Android 7.0 or newer), in landscape and portrait.

Do I need WatchBridge TV to use it?

No, it works on its own. If you also use WatchBridge TV with the same email, you can send a movie, a series or a live channel from the phone to the TV, and ask the TV to record a channel.

How many channels can I watch at once?

Up to 3 in Multiview. Only one has sound; tap another screen to move the sound. Your source may limit how many channels it lets you open at the same time.
