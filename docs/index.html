Technical details & permissions

On Google TV, WatchBridge TV uses Android's Accessibility API narrowly: it only watches for clicks on the launcher's recommendation cards and sees that click's payload — nothing else on screen. Every other click — app icons, menus, anything that isn't a recommendation — is left completely alone. Titles are looked up on TMDB with a bundled key, so there's nothing to set up.

Fire TV mode

Fire OS blocks this for every sideloaded app. We built around it anyway.

Fire OS disables the standard Android Accessibility method for any app not installed from the Amazon Appstore — the same method every recommendation-redirect app depends on. The usual workaround is temporarily rooting your Fire TV Stick and replacing Fire OS's own launcher with Google TV's.

WatchBridge TV doesn't ask you to do any of that. Fire TV mode reads the recommendation's title straight off the screen using on-device text recognition — nothing recorded leaves the device. Grant Usage access and a one-time screen-recording permission, and it runs from your actual Fire TV home screen, no root, no launcher swap.

Matching

Asks once. Never asks again.

When two titles share an exact name but not a year — a remake, say — WatchBridge TV asks which one you meant instead of guessing.

Pick once, and it's remembered: the same title never has to ask twice. And when one candidate is obviously the popular pick — a new release next to a decades-old obscurity — it skips the question entirely.

Turn it off from Settings → "Ask when a match is ambiguous" if you'd rather it always pick automatically.

FAQ

Questions people ask

Is WatchBridge a streaming service?

No. WatchBridge doesn't provide, host or sell any movies or series. It opens the title in a player app you already use.

Does it replace my Google TV home screen?

No. It works alongside your launcher and only reacts when you click a recommendation card.

Which players does it work with?

Nuvio, Stremio, Plex, Jellyfin, WuPlay and Wholphin — plus SmartTube or TizenTube Cobalt for YouTube on Google TV. You can switch any time in Settings.

Is there a subscription?

No. After a 2-day free trial it's a single €4.99 payment.

How many TVs can I use it on?

One license covers up to 5 devices, and your phone or tablet doesn't use up a TV slot.

Does it work on Fire TV?

Yes, through Fire TV mode, with no root. It isn't instant like Google TV: you hold on a card for a couple of seconds and confirm with OK.

Is there a free trial?

Yes — 2 days with everything unlocked, and no card needed.

Is it on Google Play?

A Google Play version is in closed testing and heading for public release. Until then you can install the APK, which works the same way.

Does it include TV channels? Which IPTV lists work?

No channels or lists are included — you bring your own. WatchBridge accepts an M3U/M3U8 link, an Xtream Codes login (server, username, password) or an .m3u file, and shows them as a TV guide.

Ready when your TV is

Any Google TV device — Chromecast with Google TV, or a Google TV edition from Sony, TCL, Hisense — or any Fire TV device, via Fire TV mode above. Plus one of the six player apps already installed.
