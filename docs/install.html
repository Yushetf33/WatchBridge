Install guide

Install WatchBridge TV

Click a recommendation on your Google TV, Fire TV, or Android TV launcher and it opens straight in Nuvio, Stremio, WuPlay, Plex, Jellyfin, or Wholphin. It's on Google Play — or install it manually with whichever option below fits you best.

Using Downloader

TV only

  1. On the TV, install Downloader from Google Play (the app is called "Downloader by AFTVnews").

  2. Open it and, in the URL field, type this code:

    8254947 always points to the latest version

  3. It downloads the APK and offers to install it right away.

    If Android TV warns that installing from an unknown source is blocked, you'll need to allow it for Downloader the first time — the warning itself has a button to do that.
2

With your phone

Send Files to TV

  1. On the TV, install Send Files to TV from Google Play and open it — it'll show an address or a QR code.

  2. From your phone's browser, go to that address (or scan the QR code) and download the app-release.apk file from the releases page.

  3. Once the transfer finishes, the TV will let you install it directly.

3

Using atvTools

phone, no computer

  1. On the TV: Settings → Device Preferences → About → tap "Build" 7 times to enable Developer options, then go into Developer options and turn on Network debugging. Note the IP address and port it shows.

  2. On your phone (not the TV), install atvTools from Google Play and open it. Make sure the phone is on the same Wi-Fi network as the TV.

  3. In atvTools, add the TV as a device using the IP address from step 1 to pair with it.

  4. Once paired, use atvTools' install APK option to browse to the downloaded app-release.apk and install it.

  5. To grant the special permission mentioned below: tap into WatchBridge TV in atvTools' app list, open the Permisos tab, scroll down to android.permission.WRITE_SECURE_SETTINGS, and flip its toggle on (not "Write settings", which is a different permission).

    On devices like the Google TV Streamer, where the "Allow restricted setting" toggle isn't shown anywhere, this may not be enough on its own: Android 14 also needs the ACCESS_RESTRICTED_SETTINGS command from the ADB section below. atvTools can run ADB commands too, so no computer is needed.
4

Via ADB

from a computer

  1. Download app-release.apk from the releases page.

  2. On the TV: Settings → Device Preferences → About → tap "Build" 7 times to enable Developer options, then turn on USB debugging or Network debugging.

  3. From your computer, with adb installed:

    adb connect <tv-ip>:5555adb install app-release.apk
✓

Activate the service

once installed

  1. Open WatchBridge TV from the TV's launcher.

  2. Tap "Enable the service in Accessibility" — it takes you straight to the right screen. Enable it there.

  3. Verify your subscription with the email you paid with (or start the 2-day free trial, no card needed) — that's it, click any recommendation on the launcher to try it out.

Does the switch turn itself off, or won't your box let you enable Accessibility? Fix it here → (error codes E01–E02, including how to enable it over ADB on boxes that block it).

The toggle turns itself off

The most common things people hit installing and activating the service. Here's why they happen and how to fix them.

Play Protect is blocking the install

This happens with every app installed outside Google Play, not just this one — Play Protect can't scan an app it has no record of, so it warns you by default. If you see a warning screen, look for "More details" and then "Install anyway". If it blocks the install completely with no way through, open the Play Store app → Settings → Play Protect → the gear icon, and turn off "Scan apps with Play Protect", then try installing again — you can turn it back on right after.

Why does this happen?

Since Android 13, the system blocks any app installed outside Google Play from enabling Accessibility by default — it's an Android protection ("Restricted Settings"), not a bug in the app.

How do I unblock it?

Go to Settings → Apps → WatchBridge TV and look for an option along the lines of "Allow restricted setting" (usually under a three-dot menu). Enable it, then try Accessibility again.

What if that option isn't there? (e.g. Google TV Streamer)

ADB is needed once. On Android 14 (confirmed on the Google TV Streamer) the first command is required; the second one lets the app switch Accessibility on by itself: adb shell appops set com.tunombre.tvbridge ACCESS_RESTRICTED_SETTINGS allow then adb shell pm grant com.tunombre.tvbridge android.permission.WRITE_SECURE_SETTINGS. After that, open WatchBridge and tap Turn on accessibility inside the app: it switches on by itself, no more ADB needed.

No computer? Do it from your phone with atvTools instead: pair with the TV (see the atvTools section above) and run the two commands above in its ADB shell. Or tap into WatchBridge TV in its app list, open the Permisos tab and flip on android.permission.WRITE_SECURE_SETTINGS (not "Write settings", a different permission) — but on the Google TV Streamer the first command is still needed.

Full error-code reference (E01–E06), plus Fire TV mode and voice search setup, is in the setup & troubleshooting guide.
