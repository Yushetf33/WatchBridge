Launchers

Your rows, on the launcher you use

WatchBridge adds its own rows to the home screen of Projectivy Launcher and Monet, and opens other apps’ cards in your player.

Projectivy Launcher Monet Google TV launcher

01Projectivy Launcher

Rows on the Projectivy home screen

Your rows
Favorite and recent IPTV channels, Continue watching, Movies for you and Series for you, with full posters.
Other apps’ cards
Press a card from another app’s row (a Prime Video title, for example) and it opens in the player you chose.
Needs
WatchBridge TV 1.8.15 or later.

Turn it on

  1. Open the gear in Projectivy.

  2. Choose Edit channels, then WatchBridge TV.

  3. Set each row you want to Visible.

Tested only with Prime Video. Other streaming platforms may publish empty rows or cards that cannot be redirected. If something does not work for you, write to contacto@watchbridge.app and tell us which app and launcher you use.

02Monet

Wide images for Monet’s cards

Your rows
Movies for you and Series for you use wide images that fill Monet’s cards. The app switches by itself when you open Monet or Projectivy.
Other apps’ cards
Recommendations from other apps (for example Prime Video) open in your app.
Needs
WatchBridge TV 1.8.18 or later.

Turn it on

  1. Open WatchBridge TV and go to Settings.

  2. Turn on Voice search.

Tested only with Prime Video. Titles that TMDb has not linked to IMDb yet (very new releases) cannot be opened in your player. If something does not work for you, write to contacto@watchbridge.app and tell us which app and launcher you use.
