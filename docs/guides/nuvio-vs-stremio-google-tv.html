Nuvio vs Stremio on Google TV: Which Should You Use? (2026)

If you are choosing between Nuvio and Stremio for Google TV, the honest answer is that they are closer than they look: both organize add-ons into a browsable movies and shows interface. This guide covers what they share, where they differ, and what WatchBridge TV does with each one. It is not a review: which one suits you depends on your devices and your own taste.

The short answer

Both are add-on based media browsers. Nuvio follows the same add-on format as Stremio, so an add-on link that works in one generally works in the other, and many people keep the same add-ons in both. Where they differ is mostly around the edges: how each one is distributed, which devices it runs on, and how the interface feels.

The last row is the one that matters for this site: from WatchBridge TV's side the two are functionally identical. A recommendation is identified through TMDB and its exact page opens directly in whichever one you set as your destination, with no searching by name.

Where they really differ

  • Distribution and updates. Stremio is the more established project, with official apps on more platforms. Nuvio is newer and the way you get it can change, so check its official page for the current builds instead of relying on an old guide, this one included. An outdated or unofficial build is also the most common reason a title does not open (see below).
  • Add-ons. They share the add-on format, but Nuvio also has plugins. If you paste an add-on link into the plugins section it will not work: add-ons and plugins are different things.
  • Interface and setup. This is personal. Some people prefer one layout, one way of managing add-ons, one remote-control feel. The only reliable test is to use each for a few days.

Using both with WatchBridge TV

You do not have to pick forever. You can change your destination player in Settings at any time, and you can keep both installed and switch between them. There is also an optional, off-by-default content type rule: one app for movies and a different one for series, instead of one app for everything.

If one doesn't open the title

  • Check the build. The app opens but lands nowhere? That is almost always an outdated or unofficial build, which sometimes does not register its deep-link scheme correctly. Update to the latest build.
  • Using a Nuvio fork? WatchBridge detects any app that handles nuvio:// links, so forks do not show "Nuvio not installed".
  • The troubleshooting guide covers the rest, by error code.

How to choose

  • Already use Stremio on other devices? Keeping Stremio on your TV means one app to learn everywhere.
  • Mostly a Google TV or Fire TV user who sets things up from the sofa? Try Nuvio too, since it is built around that kind of use.
  • Not sure? Install both, set one as your destination, and switch after a few days. It costs you one setting.
