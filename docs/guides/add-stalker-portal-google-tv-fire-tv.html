Guide

Add a Stalker (Ministra) portal to your IPTV on Google TV and Fire TV

Some IPTV providers don't give you an M3U link or an Xtream Codes login: they give you a portal address and register a MAC address for you, the way MAG set-top boxes log in. WatchBridge TV and WatchBridge Mobile can use that pair too. This feature is experimental: this guide says exactly what it does and what to check if your provider behaves differently.

What you need

  • The portal address your provider gave you, for example http://provider.com:8080/c/. It can also be given as just provider.com:8080 or ending in /stalker_portal/c/.
  • The MAC address your provider registered for you: 12 hexadecimal digits such as 00:1A:79:AB:CD:EF. Separators don't matter (:, - or none) and neither do capitals.
  • WatchBridge TV 1.8.26 or newer, or WatchBridge Mobile 1.3.18 or newer. A 2-day free trial with no card, then €4.99 once for both.
WatchBridge doesn't provide or sell any channels. A Stalker portal is your own subscription with your own provider, exactly like an M3U link or an Xtream Codes login: you bring it, WatchBridge turns it into a TV guide.

On the TV, step by step

  1. Open IPTV. If you already have a list, open More options (the three dots) → Your lists → + Add a list. With no list yet you land on this screen straight away.
  2. Choose Stalker portal among the four options (M3U link, Xtream Codes, Stalker portal, M3U file).
  3. Type the portal address and the MAC. Give the list a name if you like.
  4. Press Load list. The channels load with their categories and the guide the portal provides.

On the phone or tablet

  1. Open the IPTV tab, tap the name of your list at the top → Add a list.
  2. Choose Stalker, type the portal and the MAC, and tap Add.

How it works

WatchBridge identifies itself to the portal with your MAC, as a set-top box would, and downloads the channel list. Channels in a Stalker portal don't have a fixed link: when you open one, WatchBridge asks the portal for a temporary link and plays that, so each time you change channel it asks again. If the portal stops recognising the session, WatchBridge logs in again by itself.

Good to know

  • It is experimental. We have tested it against a test portal that behaves like Stalker, not yet against a provider's real one. Portals differ in small ways, so some may need adjustments.
  • Some providers allow one device per MAC or a limited number of simultaneous streams. If the original box is on at the same time, a channel may refuse to start.
  • The MAC is stored only on your device, in the list you create, and is sent only to the portal you typed.

If it doesn't work

  • "The portal didn't respond or doesn't accept this MAC address". Check the address (try it with and without /c/), that the MAC is exactly the one registered with your provider, and that your subscription is active.
  • The channels load but one won't play. Your provider may be limiting simultaneous connections for that MAC: turn off the other box or try again in a minute. WatchBridge shows "Couldn't get the stream from the portal" when the portal doesn't give a link.
  • No guide. Not every portal publishes one. The channels still work; the rows say there is no guide.
  • Still failing? Tell us through the contact page which type of portal it is (never send your MAC or credentials) so we can adjust it.
