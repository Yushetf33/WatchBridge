How to add an M3U or Xtream Codes list on Google TV and Fire TV

Your provider gave you an M3U link, an Xtream Codes login or a file. Here is how to turn any of them into a TV guide on Google TV, Android TV or Fire TV with WatchBridge TV.

Before you start

  • WatchBridge TV installed on your TV — see the install guide.
  • Your own list from your IPTV provider. WatchBridge doesn't provide or sell channels.

Add the list

  1. Open WatchBridge TV and go to IPTV.
  2. Choose how you have your list:
    • M3U link — paste the URL (it looks like https://provider.com/list.m3u). .m3u8, .m3u.gz and PLS lists also work.
    • Xtream Codes — enter the server (for example http://provider.com:8080), your username and your password. The guide URL is filled in for you.
    • M3U file — press Receive from phone or PC. The TV shows an address; open it from a phone or PC on the same Wi-Fi, choose your .m3u file and send it. No file picker needed on the TV.
  3. Optional: if your provider gives you a separate XMLTV guide link (an .xml or .xml.gz file), paste it in the EPG field. It is matched to your channels by id or by name.
  4. Press Load list.

Add more lists or refresh

  • The Lists button lets you keep several lists, switch between them and add or remove them. Each list keeps its own favorites and locks.
  • Refresh list reloads the channels and the guide.

If the list doesn't load

  • “Could not download the list (error …)”. The link is wrong, expired or blocked. Open it in a browser on the same network to check it.
  • “This list has no channels”. The URL loads something that is not an M3U list. Check you copied the whole link.
  • The guide is empty. See IPTV guide (EPG) not showing.

Then

Mark favorites, filter by group, try Multiview or say a channel name with voice search. The full list is on the IPTV page.
