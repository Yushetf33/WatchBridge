IPTV Multiview on Google TV and Fire TV: how it works and what it needs

Multiview shows two channels side by side — handy for two matches or a game and the news. Here is how to start it in WatchBridge TV on Google TV or Fire TV, and what it needs to work.

How to start Multiview

  1. Play a channel from the IPTV guide.
  2. Go to the second channel and hold OK on any programme in its row.
  3. Choose Multiview: watch next to …. Both channels appear side by side.

Controls

  • Left / Right choose which one plays sound.
  • OK takes the selected channel full screen.
  • Up / Down change the channel in the focused picture.
  • Back leaves Multiview.

What it needs

  • A source that allows two streams at once. Some providers give you a single connection; then the second channel can't open. Channels on the same multiplex often work on a single tuner box.
  • A device that can decode both pictures. WatchBridge uses the TV's hardware decoder, and includes its own software decoder for MPEG-2 channels so devices without a second hardware decoder can still show them. Sound for MPEG audio channels is also decoded in software when the device lacks it.

If the second channel doesn't open

  • Check how many simultaneous connections your provider allows. One connection means one channel at a time.
  • Try two channels from the same provider group or multiplex.
  • If your device can't show that format next to the other picture, WatchBridge says so on screen instead of leaving a black cell.
WatchBridge doesn't provide channels. Multiview works with the list you bring; availability of channels and simultaneous streams is up to your provider.
