IPTV player for Google TV and Fire TV: what to look for

An IPTV list is just a long list of channels. What makes it pleasant to watch is the player around it: a real TV guide, your favorites first, and controls that work with a remote. This is what to look for on Google TV, Android TV and Fire TV — and how WatchBridge TV does it.

What a good IPTV player does

  • A real EPG grid. Channels on one side, programmes on a timeline, what is airing now highlighted, now and next on every row.
  • Favorites and groups. Hundreds of channels are unusable without a way to get to yours in two clicks.
  • Several lists. Many people have more than one provider or list; each should keep its own favorites.
  • Catch-up. Press a programme that already aired and watch it from the start — when your provider keeps an archive.
  • Parental control. A PIN that hides channels or whole groups from the guide.
  • Remote-first controls. Zapping, last channel and search that work without a keyboard.
  • Multiview. Two channels on screen at once, which is handy for sport.

Coming from TiviMate or a similar app?

You use the same ingredients: the M3U link or Xtream Codes login your provider gave you, and an optional XMLTV guide link. Add them in WatchBridge's IPTV screen and the guide builds itself. WatchBridge is not affiliated with TiviMate or any other IPTV app.

It is also more than an IPTV player: the same app opens Google TV and Fire TV recommendations in Nuvio, Stremio, Plex or Jellyfin, so one install covers your movies and your live TV.

What to know before you start

WatchBridge doesn't provide, host or sell any channels or lists. You bring your own, exactly as in any other IPTV player.
  • IPTV is included in WatchBridge TV on Google Play and in the APK.
  • Catch-up works only on channels where your provider keeps an archive.
  • Multiview needs a source that allows two streams at once.
